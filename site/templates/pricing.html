How it works

Flat-rate tiers

Your node count determines your tier, and that tier's per-node rate applies to all your nodes. A 500-node fleet pays 500 × $132. Simple, predictable, no cumulative math.

All features, every tier

Spanner federation, AI assistant, compliance export, RBAC, all 72 resource types. A license unlocks a node count — nothing else changes.

Grace period

If you exceed your node limit: 15 days of warnings, then 15 days where only new enrollments are blocked, then shutdown. Existing nodes are never locked out.

Source-available agent

The code that runs as root on your machines is readable and auditable under the Elastic License 2.0.
