## Tech Debt

### DryRun Handler (Advisory)
`server/grpc/admin.go` lines 267-322: The DryRun handler iterates modules and resources without calling `evaluateConditionalVars`, `ShouldIncludeModule`, or `ShouldRunResource`. This means `gvctl preview` will show planned actions for resources that would be conditionally skipped during a real check-in. Not a v1.2 requirement — flagged for future work.
