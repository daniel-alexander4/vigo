Compliance Waivers

Active waivers from envoys-tree compliance.vgo files. Waived controls count as accepted in compliance scoring.

Enforcement types:
  • enforced + waived — A configcrate actively enforces this control and a waiver is in place. The control is covered by policy regardless of the waiver. If the waiver expires, compliance scoring is unaffected.
  • waived only — No configcrate enforces this control. The waiver is the only reason it counts as accepted. If the waiver expires, this control becomes a gap in compliance scoring.
